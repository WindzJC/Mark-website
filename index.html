From the archive / 004

A city.
A poem.
A reckoning.

In 1988, Mark wrote A Crime Revisited — a poem about Dallas and the long shadow cast by President John F. Kennedy's assassination. The Sixth Floor Museum at Dealey Plaza preserves a typed copy. This history is part of his writing story, not a claim that the museum endorses his book.

The man behind the words / 005

Not a hero.
A human being.

Dallas-born and Farmers Branch-raised, Mark describes a winding route through practical work and writing. His early ventures included winning a poetry contest in college and writing for an unrealized cartoon project. He kept collecting thoughts along the way.

His published author biography resists any glamorous account of that life. The site should honor that straightforward voice: the work, the humor, the wear, the words.
